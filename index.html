Order-status questions, answered before they reach a person

"Where is my order?" is a question your shipping data already answers. ChattyBong reads that data and replies in the customer's own channel. Anything unusual, such as a damaged item or a wrong address, goes to a human with the order history attached.

We're early, so this shows how the workflow is designed to behave, not a published customer benchmark. Ask for a walkthrough using your own ticket data.

Questions teams ask first

Do you train on our data?

No. Your conversations and documents are used to answer your customers, not to train shared models. We share full data-handling details during onboarding.

What happens when the bot doesn't know the answer?

It hands the conversation to someone on your team with the full thread and any relevant records attached.

Are you SOC 2 or ISO certified?

Not yet. We're early-stage. If compliance is a blocker, tell us on the call and we'll walk you through our current security practices and roadmap.

Can we try it before committing?

Yes. We run a short pilot on your own data before any contract.

See it running on your own tickets

15 minutes, no slide deck. We connect a sandbox to a sample of your real queries and show you what it handles and what it doesn't.
